Can I get a refund if I cancel the Subscription?
No.
How do I Resolve the issue if my transaction has failed and the money got deducted from my bank account?
You have to submit the UTR No/Transaction Id and screenshot of the payment details to customer care.
What Payment methods are accepted?
UPI, Net Banking, Debit Card & Credit Card
Can I change my preferred location?
Yes! You can change it in the Dashboard
Can I change my job interests in the application?
No, you can request by contacting the customer support.
How do I know whether I have short-listed or not?
You will receive a message to your registered number or you can check in My Jobs for the status and contact the Employers.
How many jobs can I apply?
There is no limit for no. of jobs to apply. We suggest you to check your availability before applying
How can I change Email/Contact number after Registration?
Contact Customer Support.
How do I upgrade my plan?
You can contact customer support or at the time of renewal.
Where can I check whether the job posted by me is successfully posted or not?
You can check on the Jobs Posted Tab.
How can I edit the end date after posting a job?
You can go to Jobs Posted tab & edit the dates.
How can I know that someone has applied for the job?
You can know by clicking on applicant’s tab and check no. of students applied for the job.
Can I post a job other than the 50 Part-Time jobs you provided?
Yes, you can. Contact Customer Support.

What we do
ampmjobs.in is your dedicated platform for Part-Time, Seasonal, and Internship jobs, tailored exclusively for the Student community. We bridge the gap between Employers and Students, offering a wealth of Part-Time job opportunities across various locations and domains. Our "EARN WHILE YOU LEARN" initiative spans PAN India, empowering Students to gain valuable experience while supporting their studies and meeting their financial needs. Employers benefit from access to a database of eager Students, helping to eradicate child labour and providing a flexible workforce ready to meet their needs.

ampmjobs.in role
At ampmjobs.in, we maximize the benefits of a Part-Time jobs platform, offering a robust, scalable, and secure application. Students can easily register, search for jobs that interest them, and benefit from a user-friendly interface. Employers can post job requirements, search for potential hires, and manage listings with ease. Our low and affordable registration process ensures that both Students and Employers can seamlessly engage with the platform.

    Student Benefits
  • 24/7 Online Access: Search for Part-Time jobs anytime, anywhere, without the need for bulletin boards or advertisements.
  • Access to More Jobs: Explore a wider range of job opportunities across multiple locations.
  • Flexibility for Multi-tasking Students: Find Part-Time jobs in any city, even during vacations.
  • 2-Way Job Search: Create Student profiles for Employers to view and choose from.
  • E-Notifications: Receive direct alerts of new job postings.
  • Hot Postings: Always find the latest job listings, managed by Employers.
  • Secure Experience: Confidential information is only accessible to registered Employers, with alerts for profile views.
  • Searchable Job Listings: Filter listings to find specific job types easily.
